The one-liner 01 — Summary

A brand's reputation is being written right now — whether you're watching or not.

Pulse turns Sociafy from a tool that publishes your content into one that also defends it. It continuously listens wherever people talk about you, scores the sentiment, and lets your AI reply on the brand's behalf — automatically for the easy stuff, with you in the loop for anything sensitive. One dashboard tells you, at a glance, what every platform thinks of you and what customers actually keep saying.

Why it matters 02 — Problem

Reputation is everywhere, all at once — and founders can't be.

Sociafy's users are solo founders and small teams. They already can't keep up with posting — keeping up with what people say back is hopeless. So the moments that decide a brand's reputation slip by unanswered.

01

It's scattered across a dozen places.

Comments and DMs on six networks, a Google review here, a Trustpilot star there, a Reddit thread you never even saw. No single inbox. No single mood.

02

Speed is the whole game.

A complaint answered in five minutes is a save. The same complaint answered next week is a screenshot in someone's "avoid this brand" thread. Silence is the worst possible reply.

03

The tools that do this cost like enterprise.

Social-listening suites are priced for marketing departments, not founders. Pulse brings the capability to the people who feel each review most — at Sociafy prices.

What it is 03 — The loop

One continuous loop: listen, understand, respond, report.

Pulse runs the same agentic loop Sociafy already runs for publishing — pointed in the other direction. Every few minutes it sweeps your sources, reasons about what it found, acts within the guardrails you set, and folds the result back into your dashboard.

Make sense of it 05 — Understand

Every mention gets read, scored, and triaged.

Before anything is shown to you, each item passes through the same model layer Sociafy uses for composing — only here it's reading instead of writing.

Signal 01 · Sentiment

Positive, neutral, or negative

With a confidence and a short reason, so the dashboard math is honest and you can trust the rollups.

Signal 02 · Intent

Praise · question · complaint · crisis

What does this person actually want? A thank-you, an answer, a fix, or a fire to be put out — each routes differently.

Signal 03 · Reach & severity

How loud, how dangerous

Follower count, upvotes, review weight and thread velocity combine into a single reputation-threat score per mention.

Signal 04 · Theme

What it's about

Clustered into recurring topics — pricing, support, onboarding, a specific bug — so patterns surface, not just one-offs.

Answer in your voice 06 — Respond

Tiered by risk — the same dial you already trust on Autopilot.

Pulse inherits Sociafy's brand voice, knowledge base, and the same threshold model that governs auto-publishing. You set how much you trust it; it never crosses the line you draw.

Auto · green

Replies on its own, instantly

Low-risk, high-confidence items — genuine praise, simple repeat questions, "where do I sign up?" Pulse answers in brand voice within seconds and logs it.

e.g.  ★★★★★ review · "love the new dashboard"  →  warm thank-you, posted automatically.
Draft · amber

Writes the reply, you approve in one tap

Anything nuanced — a complaint, a refund ask, a factual correction. Pulse drafts the best response it can and waits for your tap. Edit or send.

e.g.  "support never replied"  →  apology + concrete next step drafted, held for your OK.
Escalate · red

Stops, alerts you, suggests a holding line

Legal threats, a viral thread, a safety claim, anything high-reach and hostile. Pulse never auto-sends these — it pings you, proposes a careful holding response, and the human decides.

e.g.  rising r/SaaS thread alleging a billing scam  →  instant alert + suggested first response.

GUARDRAILS   Brand-safety filters, a never-promise list, tone matching, and per-source caps. One slider sets the threshold — drafts-only at one end, fully autonomous at the other — exactly like Autopilot's autoPublishThreshold.

Why it's cheap to build 08 — Fit

Pulse is mostly assembly, not invention.

Almost every piece already exists in Sociafy. Pulse points the same machinery at the inbound side.

Platform adaptersThe connect / token / webhook layer that already publishes is reused to read comments, DMs and mentions. New work is the inbound endpoints, not the auth.
Autopilot thresholdThe risk dial is the existing autoPublishThreshold model, re-skinned for replies. Same mental model your users already learned.
Compose engineBrand voice, knowledge base and the variant generator that writes posts now writes replies — same prompts, same guardrails.
Credits ledgerListening is near-free; classification and AI replies meter through the existing credit system — no new billing rails.
Cron + activity logThe scheduled-publish cron becomes a scheduled-sweep cron; mentions and replies flow into the activity feed users already watch.
Net-newReview-site & Reddit connectors, the sentiment/triage pass, the mentions data model, and the Pulse dashboard. That's the build.
