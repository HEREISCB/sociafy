05 LinkedIn enrichment

"Enrichment" means: given a LinkedIn URL, an email, or a name + company, return structured profile data — current title, employer, work history, education, skills, location, connection counts, recent posts and engagement, and (sometimes) contact info. "Per-post enrichment" returns a profile's recent posts / articles with reactions and comments — useful for competitor monitoring.

Provider Per profile Per post Notes
Proxycurl $0.022 / call (3 credits) bundled in profile (last 10) API-first, ToS-aware, PAYG from $11
Phantombuster $56 — $128 / month execution-minutes metered Uses your LinkedIn cookie — account-risk
Apify (LinkedIn actors) $0.0061 / profile (mass) from $5.50 / 1k posts Third-party actors; quality varies
RapidAPI LinkedIn endpoints $0.011 — $0.055 varies per vendor Aggregator; vendor-dependent SLAs
Bright Data $0.055 / profile similar tier, per-record Enterprise scale; ToS pushed onto buyer
Apollo.io $54 — $164 / user / mo n/a B2B contact DB, not a post / activity scraper
Legal note. LinkedIn's User Agreement prohibits scraping. hiQ v. LinkedIn (9th Circuit) found that scraping public profile data is not a CFAA violation, but LinkedIn still pursues civil claims (breach of contract, tortious interference) and bans accounts. Proxycurl and Bright Data position themselves as "public data only" services — GDPR / CCPA obligations on retention, consent (EU profiles especially), and downstream use remain ours. Cookie-based tools like Phantombuster put the operator's own LinkedIn account at restriction or ban risk.

06 Platform publishing APIs X requires paid tier

Most platforms (Facebook Pages, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube) are free at our usage volume. X / Twitter is the exception — Elon's pricing overhaul put posting behind a paid subscription. The fee is flat per month, not per call, so the per-post unit cost depends on volume.

X / Twitter — required tier

Tier Monthly Write quota / mo Unit cost at quota
Free $0 1,500 posts $0
Basic recommended $220 50,000 posts $0.0044
Pro $5,500 1,000,000 posts $0.0061
Enterprise contact sales custom —
Treat the X tier as fixed infra cost, not per-post. Whether 1 user posts once or 100 users post 500 times each, the $220 Basic fee is the same. Roll it into your monthly server / infra line — once you cross ~5,000 X posts/mo across all users you're under $0.005/post, comparable to image generation. Under that threshold your effective X cost-per-post is whatever $220 ÷ actual-volume works out to.
